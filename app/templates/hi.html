What Spotter works out

Spotter works out every number in code, from what you logged.

Your assistant is told to quote them. The numbers it may estimate are a food's calories, protein, carbs and fat, and it must say so.

  • Bests. Each weighted set of {{ works.epley_min|show("count") }} to {{ works.epley_max|show("count") }} reps gives an estimated one-rep max, by the Epley formula. A new best beats every earlier set of that lift, by that estimate or by weight. A hold counts by time, a carry by load, a band by reps and help by less of it. Your lift now is your best session of the last {{ works.recent_days|show("count") }} days, or your latest before that. One light day does not pull it down.
  • Body weight now. The average of your weigh-ins in the last {{ works.average_days|show("count") }} days, or your latest when that week has none.
  • The weight trend. A smoothed line through every weigh-in: each one moves it a tenth of the way. Its rate a week needs a weigh-in in the last {{ works.rate_latest_days|show("count") }} days and one from {{ works.rate_from|show("count") }} to {{ works.rate_to|show("count") }} days ago.
  • Goal pace. Each goal shows the pace a week it needs to make its date, and your pace so far. Your pace so far is a straight line fitted through the last {{ works.fit_weeks|show("count") }} weeks, from at least {{ works.fit_points|show("count") }} points on {{ works.fit_days|show("count") }} days.
  • Where you land. That line gives the day you reach the target at this pace. It says on track when the line reaches the target by your date, and behind when it does not. There is no day when the line is flat, points away, or gets there more than {{ works.max_years|show("count") }} years out.
  • The plan line. A straight line from your start to your target, by their dates. It says how far ahead of it or behind it you are today.
  • Calories. Your average a day over the last {{ works.week_days|show("count") }} and {{ works.window_days|show("count") }} days, counting only days with food logged. It says how much of that is estimates.
  • Cardio calories. From the activity's MET value, the minutes and your own body weight now. With no weigh-in there is no estimate.
  • Maintenance. About what you eat to stay the same weight: your food, less your weight change, over {{ works.maintenance_days|show("count") }} days. It needs food on {{ works.maintenance_logged|show("count") }} of those days, a weigh-in in the last {{ works.maintenance_latest|show("count") }} days and one from {{ works.maintenance_from|show("count") }} to {{ works.maintenance_to|show("count") }} days ago. It is an estimate, and it never changes your target.

What it does not do

  • It gives no medical advice, and knows nothing of injury, pain, form or recovery.
  • It takes no stones, and no inches for a body size. Weights are kg or lb and distances km or miles: say which you use.
  • It counts calories, protein, carbs and fat, and no other nutrient such as fibre or salt.
  • It knows lifts by its own list of {{ keeps.lifts|show("count") }} names. A plain word such as squat takes the usual lift, or the one you have logged. A name Spotter does not know is refused, never guessed.
  • It sends no reminders and no email, and does not sync with a phone or a watch.
